About the game

The Backrooms is a first-person horror game about walking through the wrong parts of a building. You can go alone or bring up to seven other people.

The yellow halls are the quiet part. Nothing attacks you there right away. The longer you stay, the more you start to notice the hum.

Your torch runs down. Your camcorder lets you read signs from far away. Most of what hunts you, you can't fight, so you mostly have to get away.

Who you'll meet

Things that were people, or near enough.

Subdivision A

Empty rooms

Wallpaper, carpet, lights that buzz. Nothing will hurt you in here at first. It's the thing you keep noticing that you should listen to.

Bacteria

Mimics of people

Bodies stitched back together by a bacteria that keeps them moving. They copy voices. A call for help from the next room is usually a trap.

Still Lifes

Copies

Copies of people and everyday objects, made by the place itself. They don't blink. They can flip a light switch while you are standing right next to it.

Null Zones

Gaps

Spots in the floor you can't see. People walk into them and keep walking. The ones who come back are usually late, sometimes by months.

Your job

Catch it. Log it. Get out.

01

Catch

Something in the halls is wrong: a room that doesn't match the last one, a sound with no source, a face that is a little too still. Catch it before it catches you.

02

Scan

Hold the field scanner on it to detect the anomaly and log its details. The log can disagree with what you see. Trust the mismatch.

03

Report

File each anomaly in the field terminal. Reported entries are what the Threshold Spatial Research Agency learns from, and what your crew needs to know which halls to avoid.

04

Survive

Most anomalies can't be fought. Keep your torch lit, keep moving, and don't stand still to study something that is studying you back.

05

Find the way out

Every run ends with an exit, and it is never where the last hall pointed. Mark the corridors you've walked, follow the signs, and don't let the hum lead you back.

Equipment

What you carry.

8 itemsScroll the list
F

Flashlight

Turns on and off with F. It drains as you use it. Battery packs refill about 45% each.

R

AA battery pack

Found on the floor. Press R to load one into the flashlight.

X

Camcorder

Hold X and scroll to zoom up to 4x. Both hands are on it while it's up, so you can't use anything else.

G

Camera flash

Two per run. A direct hit blinds the Bacteria for a few seconds. A miss is loud.

T

Hazard tape

Hold it on a wall or the floor and look along it to pull a strip off. Use it to mark corridors you've already walked.

Q

Field scanner

Hold it on an entity to log it. The log doesn't always match what's in front of you.

B

Marker

Hold B on a wall, floor or ceiling and drag to draw a line. N changes the colour. Hold H on a line to rub it out.

Tab

Field terminal

Your items, dossier, entries, papers, clearance and crew. F1 to F6 switch between pages.

Co-op

Up to eight people.

One player hosts and shares a room code. Everyone else types it in. Voice is proximity-based, so you hear the people near you and lose the rest around corners.

The Mimic copies where someone walked a few minutes ago, step for step, and wears their face. It never looks like you. The field scanner is the only way to tell them apart.
